ABOUT US — 02

Why Brightix exists, and how we work.

Four questions, answered in order: what we do, why we do it, how we approach it, and where it is going.

01

The problem

0M

Security roles unfilled globally — capability, not budget, is the constraint.

0%

Of breaches involve a human factor: phishing, error, misconfiguration.

02

What we do

Educate

Structured security curricula, labs and assessments for students and staff.

Engineer

Secure infrastructure, tooling and IT solutions built and maintained by our team.

Assess

Readiness reviews, exercises and advisory that tell partners where they actually stand.

03

Our approach

  1. A

    Understand the environment

    We map systems, skills and risk before recommending anything.

  2. B

    Build capability alongside systems

    Training and engineering run in parallel, not as separate contracts.

  3. C

    Prove it under pressure

    Simulated incidents and lab exercises validate the result.

  4. D

    Hand over, then stay available

    Documentation and ongoing support so improvements hold.

04

Impact & mission

MISSION

To make strong security ordinary — by training the people who practise it and building the systems that support them.

VISION

A generation of engineers for whom security is not a specialism, but a default.
